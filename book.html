THE LAST SUMMER

Some summers stay forever.

A story about a summer, a lake, two people, and the memories that never really leave.

Page 01
Chapter 01

The Boy at the Shore

The lake was quiet that afternoon. The mountains stood beyond it like silent guardians, their reflections breaking whenever the wind touched the water.

Shiv sat alone at the end of the old wooden pier. His notebook rested beside him, open to a blank page. He had brought it with him almost every day that summer, although he rarely knew what he wanted to write.

Sometimes he came here simply because the lake asked nothing from him.

Page 02

The Quiet Place

At home, everyone seemed to have an idea of who Shiv was supposed to become.

His future was discussed over dinner, during phone calls, and sometimes in conversations that were supposed to be about something else.

Study. Career. Family. Responsibility.

The words were not cruel, but they were heavy. Shiv understood why his family cared about him. Still, there were days when he wondered whether anyone had ever asked him what he actually wanted.

Page 03

A Presence

He looked across the lake.

For a moment, everything seemed exactly as it had been for the last few weeks.

Then he noticed someone standing near the shore.

A girl.

She wasn't doing anything remarkable. She was simply looking at the water, as if she had forgotten the rest of the world existed.

Shiv looked away first.

Page 04

The First Glance

He tried to return to his notebook.

But the blank page suddenly felt less interesting.

He wondered who she was.

The thought surprised him.

Shiv wasn't someone who usually became curious about strangers. He preferred observing people from a distance, understanding them without becoming part of their story.

Yet something about that afternoon felt different.

The Summer Begins

The girl eventually walked away.

Shiv watched her disappear into the old streets.

He closed his notebook.

He didn't know it yet, but that ordinary afternoon would become one of the memories he carried for years.

Some stories begin with a conversation.

Others begin with nothing more than a glance.

The End

Some Summers End.
Some Summers Stay Forever.

Shiv closed his notebook.

Outside, the lake reflected the last light of the evening.

He smiled—not because the summer had returned, but because he no longer needed it to.

It had already become a part of him.

And perhaps that was what some summers were meant to do.

They arrive. They change you. They leave.

And somehow, they stay forever.
