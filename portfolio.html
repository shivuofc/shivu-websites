01
EDUCATION • APP DEVELOPMENT

Study Pilot

An ambitious education app designed to become a smart study companion for students.

Study Pilot was one of my biggest ideas. The goal was to create a single platform where students could find useful study resources, organize their preparation and get help while studying.

The original vision included support for HBSE and NCERT students from Classes 6 to 12. I wanted the app to bring different parts of the student journey together instead of making students search through multiple places for everything.

The planned experience included an AI study assistant, NCERT resources, HBSE syllabus, previous-year questions, notes, quizzes, practice tests, study planning and offline functionality.

I also wanted Study Pilot to work well on lower-end phones and slower internet connections. The idea was not just to make something visually attractive, but something genuinely useful for students who may not have expensive devices or unlimited data.

THE VISION

One place for studying.

The long-term vision was to make studying more organized by combining resources, practice and intelligent assistance into one simple experience.

WHAT I LEARNED

Ideas need execution.

Building an app is very different from imagining one. I learned about UI design, application structure, data handling, technical limitations and the amount of work required to turn an idea into a reliable product.

WHY I PAUSED IT

A temporary pause, not an ending.

Study Pilot had to be paused before I could complete the full vision.

One major reason was the financial side of development. Building and maintaining a complete application can require hosting, backend services, databases, development tools and other resources. At that stage, I did not have enough budget to keep everything running and develop it at the level I wanted.

There were also technical issues that made development more difficult than I expected. Some parts of the system needed more testing, better architecture and more reliable implementation before they could become a proper public product.

Instead of releasing something unfinished and unreliable, I decided to step back, learn more and return to the project with a stronger foundation.

WHAT'S NEXT

Study Pilot is not forgotten.

I still want to complete Study Pilot. The project taught me too much to simply abandon it.

I plan to return to it when I have the required resources, stronger technical knowledge and a better development setup. The goal will be to rebuild the important parts properly instead of simply continuing from where I stopped.

The project is paused — not cancelled. I hope to complete it soon.

02
CONTENT CREATION

Visuals & Content

I have spent a lot of time experimenting with visual content, short-form videos, designs, edits and social-media focused ideas.

The interesting part of visual creation for me has never been only the final image or video. I enjoy thinking about the complete experience: the first impression, the typography, the composition, the mood, the pacing and the story behind the content.

Social media also taught me how quickly people decide whether something is worth watching. That pushed me to understand hooks, presentation, consistency and audience attention.

Some experiments worked well. Others didn't. But every experiment gave me information about what I should improve next.

Visual creation is still an ongoing part of my journey, and I want to keep improving the quality and originality of everything I publish.

03
THE LAST SUMMER

My Storytelling Journey

A personal attempt to turn emotions, memories and imagination into a complete story.

Writing The Last Summer became a completely different kind of creative experience for me. Unlike a social-media post that can be created and forgotten quickly, a book requires patience, consistency and attention to the smallest details.

The story follows emotional moments, relationships, memories, conversations and the feeling of a summer that becomes much more meaningful than it initially appears.

I wanted the book to feel cinematic while still feeling personal. The goal was to make readers remember the atmosphere of the story rather than simply remember its events.

Writing also taught me that creativity does not always arrive when you want it to. Sometimes a paragraph works immediately. Sometimes an idea needs to be rewritten several times.

That process has made The Last Summer more than just a writing project. It has become a record of how my creative thinking has developed.

05
SOCIAL MEDIA • LEARNING

Growth Lab

A collection of things I have learned while experimenting with content creation and social media growth.

Growth Lab started from my own curiosity about how creators grow online.

I experimented with different types of content, hooks, editing styles, posting strategies, audience behaviour and ways of presenting an account.

Over time, I realized that growth is not simply about getting one viral video. Sustainable improvement comes from understanding your audience, creating consistently, testing ideas and learning from results.

Growth Lab is where I want to organize those lessons into something easier to understand. Instead of presenting growth as a magic formula, the goal is to explain the creative and strategic thinking behind content.

Topics can include content ideas, hooks, presentation, consistency, audience retention, experimentation and building a recognizable creator identity.

Most importantly, I want Growth Lab to represent learning rather than pretending to know everything. Social media changes constantly, so there is always something new to test.
